=====================================================================
 ROTA - DEMO VOICEOVER, FORMATTED FOR TEXT-TO-SPEECH
 For fish.audio/app/text-to-speech
=====================================================================

310 words - down from 412. At a slow 110 words per minute that is 2:45;
at a normal 140 it is 2:09.

If your generated total still comes out over 2:50, don't re-record. Say
so, and the video build applies a two-to-three percent speed-up, which
is inaudible.

HOW TO USE

Generate SEVEN separate clips, one per block. Paste only the text between
the dashed lines - not the "BLOCK n" headings.

Seven clips rather than one is deliberate: it gives an exact measured
length for each beat, so the screen recording is cut to your pacing
instead of the other way round. A bad take also only costs one block.

Save them as:  beat1.mp3  beat2.mp3  ...  beat7.mp3
WAV works too.

VOICE SETTINGS

- A calm, level narrator. This is a product explanation, not an advert.
- Normal speed. You no longer need to rush.
- If the total still comes out over 2:50, say so - the video build can
  speed the audio by a few percent, which is inaudible.

IF A WORD COMES OUT WRONG

- "Web MCP" should sound like "web em-see-pee". If it garbles it, try
  "Web M C P" or "Web M.C.P."
- "rota" should rhyme with "motor". If it says "rot-uh", replace every
  "rota" with "roster" - same meaning, and the word never appears on
  screen.
- Marco is MAR-ko. Sofia is so-FEE-ah. Liam is LEE-am.

=====================================================================
 WHEN YOU HAVE THE AUDIO
=====================================================================

Drop the files into  media/  in this repo:

    media/beat1.mp3 ... media/beat7.mp3      (preferred)
    or  media/voiceover.mp3                  (single block)

Then say so, and:

    npm run video

drives the live app through the demo on a timeline derived from the real
length of each clip, screen-records it, and muxes your audio in.

Output: media/rota-demo.mp4, ready to upload.
